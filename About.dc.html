Capsule headline and orbiting photos

The headline reads as a normal sentence in one weight. Only "design" sits in a gradient pill and "programming" in a 2px ink outline, and a hand-drawn loop in the gradient's sky blue, #74b0df, draws itself around "on purpose." over 1.4s when the page loads.

Seven 76px photo circles share one dotted orbit, evenly spaced, and pass behind the words. Each has a faint 1px outline so the lighter photos keep a clean edge on the cream. When the page loads, each one fades in and drifts 40 degrees along the orbit into place over 1.2s, 90ms after the one before it. After that, scrolling turns the whole orbit together at 0.12 degrees per pixel, and they never drift or bounce on their own. Hovering or tapping a photo grows it 1.5 times. Captions are off for now, and one shows under its photo as soon as it has text. On phones they become one row under the headline, and each one fades up 14px into place.

Logo animation on Home

On load, the three loops of the real logo, outlined in cream on the gradient, fade in a little apart at the end of the line under the name, then come together as the mark. They fade in from 120ms over 500ms and move together from 800ms over 700ms on the shared easing curve. With reduced motion, the mark is simply there. The Home hero now shows the full-colour logo on a white tile in its place, so this animation isn't on any page right now.

The loops come straight from logo-14.svg, split by colour into their three strokes, so nothing is redrawn.
