/ Components

These are the parts every page is built from. Each one shows its states side by side, and the strip at the top of this sheet is the live nav.

Nav

The nav is a sticky, faintly frosted strip with no divider line. Links are grey until they're hovered, the current page turns ink and semibold, and the last link's text lines up with the right edge of the grid.

Below 540px wide, the name beside the mark is hidden so the four links still fit on one line. It stays in the link's label for screen readers.

Hero window

The gradient window sits 4px below the nav. The name spans its top, and under it everything sits on a widget grid of square cells with 12px gaps and 24px padding, sized by the box's width through container queries. The box has six columns from 880px wide, four from 520px and two below that. Widgets come in three shapes, small, medium and large, so the grid never leaves a gap.

The intro sits straight on the gradient over the deep blue. The widgets are light glass with ink text, 20px corners and 16px padding: music, New York weather, a photo and the logo as an app icon. Their states are on the Hero states board.

cells: (box width minus gaps) divided by 6, 4 or 2. Widgets: rgba(241,240,236,0.72) fill, 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.8) border, blur(16px) saturate(150%)

Project card

Each image keeps its whole subject. Mockups sit on their own backdrop colour, pinned to the top in cards taller than the image and centred in wider ones, and photos crop around a focal point. A glass caption sits 12px in from the bottom left with the name, an optional one-line summary and the kind, and a round glass arrow sits in the bottom right. The whole card is one link.

On hover, the image eases in by 3% and the arrow fills with ink.

Card pair

Two cards share a row at the same height, one six columns wide and one four, 12px apart. Rows alternate wide then narrow, then narrow then wide, and a last card on its own goes full width.

Filter chips

The chips sit at the top of Work. The active chip fills with ink, and the label above the grid names the current view and changes as you filter. There's no result count.

Each chip is 36px tall and carries an invisible 44px hit area.

Index list with hover preview

Hovering a row, or reaching it with the keyboard, slides the name 8px right and brings in a 240px preview of that project. Each row has its own tilt, position and height so the preview feels hand-placed, and it glides between rows over 260ms.

This list opens on Chai & Co. so the preview shows here. Touch screens and narrow screens get the plain list.

Footer

The footer ends every page on the gradient, full width with 20px top corners. The label and the availability sentence sit together on the cobalt side. The email is one button that copies itself, and the word Copied appears under it only after the click.

The live footer closing this sheet is the same component every page mounts.
