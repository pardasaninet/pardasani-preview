Split section

This is the default text layout. A small label sits in the left half, columns 1 to 5, and the content sits in the right half, columns 6 to 10, so the right half runs down every page as one clean edge. Below 720px the label stacks above the content.

/ Problem

Students need things done and don't have money to spare: cleaning, laundry pickup, watching cats, watering plants, help moving. Students also need money, but often can't commit to a regular job because the hours don't fit around classes. The informal version of this already happens on campus.

Statement

A statement is two-tone. The first sentence is ink and the rest drops to the second tone, which is only ever used at 24px and up. A statement with one sentence stays ink throughout.

Decision block

Each decision is a split section with the label on the left and a heading and short paragraph on the right. The images follow 40px below, and their layout changes from block to block so the page never settles into one rhythm.

A decision can also have no image. Then the block ends on its paragraph, with no empty plate, and the next section follows after the usual wide gap.

heading clamp(22px, 2.1vw, 28px) 700, images var(--gap-s) below

Full width at 16:9

/ Decision 01

A university email replaces the resume.

The platform has to earn trust before it can get anyone hired, so verification through a university email does the job a resume would normally do. Priya needs credibility, Jake needs speed and Aaliya needs flexibility. Any design decision that serves all three at once is the right one.

/ Decision 02

On mobile, the gig card leads with the price.

On desktop a gig card can hold more detail. On mobile the same card has to lead with the price and a one-line description, so the decision happens at a glance.

Outcomes

Outcomes replaces the reflection. It's one line in ink in the right half, then images below it, either full width or as a 6 and 4 pair.

The credits stay where they were, closing the story in the right half under a hairline, before the next project. They show Context and Skills, plus only the rows a project actually has, such as a live site, a video, a repo or an AI-imagery note. An empty row never shows.

Sticky project bar

A glass pill stays fixed 20px from the bottom of every case study, with Back to all work, a home icon, and Next on an ink fill.

Because it also floats over the cream page, it carries the 1px outline as well as the glass edge, so it never disappears into the ground. The live bar is pinned to the bottom of this sheet.
