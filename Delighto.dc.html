Delighto.ai

/ Problem

Delighto.ai makes digital business cards that professionals share with a tap or scan. Before the redesign, the brand had a minimal website, no companion app and no social media presence. Our team of nine set out to understand users' needs and improve the product experience and reliability across web and mobile.

/ Decision 01

Research.

The team's interviews and competitor analysis showed that networking tools are fragmented and that follow-up is the biggest breakdown in networking.

/ Decision 02

Design to code.

I took the UX team's Figma file and ran it through Figma Make to generate the codebase in TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS and SVG. This put the structure and components in place quickly, so the build started from a working shell instead of a blank file.

/ Decision 03

Design fidelity.

The generated code got the structure right but lost precision in spacing and fine details. I checked it against the Figma file, corrected the output and rewrote sections where the design intent didn't survive. The final site is fully responsive, with hover and click microinteractions on buttons and cards.

/ Outcomes

The result is a live, fully responsive website and a mobile app prototype for the client.
