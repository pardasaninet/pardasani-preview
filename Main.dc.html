/ Gradient

The gradient comes from the business card: cobalt top left, sky on the right, warm taupe bottom left and pale blue bottom right. It plays the part landscape photos play on other sites, so it only appears in the home hero window, the footer, the Brand skill card, the Toolkit tile and pills that need to stand out.

A grain at 4% opacity sits on large gradient surfaces. Nothing else on the site gets texture.

--gradient

Cream text was measured against each region. Only the cobalt corner clears 4.5:1 for small text, and the sky, taupe and pale blue regions fail even the 3:1 allowed at 24px and up. So cream text stays on the cobalt side where it can. The Brand card's list sits on a soft ink layer. The other ink layers were taken out by choice, so the footer's lower half, the hero line, the Toolkit labels and the Contact block, now on light glass, sit below these targets.

/ Materials

Glass is only ever a floating layer over the gradient or an image. Light surfaces on the cream page separate with a 1px outline. Cards and tiles also carry a raised glass edge, the softest shadow underneath and a hairline of light inside, and nothing heavier than that.

/ Radii

Four radii cover everything. Photos that aren't rectangles are always perfect circles, never ovals.

Span n of 10 is calc((100% - 108px) * n / 10 + (n - 1) * 12px), and offset k is calc((100% - 108px) * k / 10 + k * 12px).

Below 720px of content width, every span goes full width and offsets drop to 0. Two-up rows stack, and small groups like the footer links go two per row. The classes c2 to c7 do this with one clamp each, so nothing needs a breakpoint.
